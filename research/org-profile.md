## 3. Programs (only what the org lists)

Menu structure: Support & Education > Support Groups / Mental Health Education / Presentations & Workshops / Wellness & Outreach - https://namicentraloregon.org/

**Support groups (peer-led)** - https://namicentraloregon.org/support-and-education/support-groups/
- NAMI Connection Peer Support Group - "for people with mental health conditions"; in-person (Bend) and online
- NAMI Family Support Group - "for family members, significant others and friends"; online "3rd Saturday"; a Redmond in-person family group also exists (https://namicentraloregon.org/series/redmond-in-person-family-support-group/ - from search listing)

- NAMI Family-to-Family - families, significant others, friends of people with mental health conditions
- NAMI Homefront - "free, 6-session" program for families/caregivers/friends of military and veterans
