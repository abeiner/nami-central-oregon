### 3.2 NAMI National rules and wording (national office only; affiliates "are separate entities")
NAMI National Board Operating Policy 8.6 "Guidelines for Business Support Relationships" (adopted March 2009) [READ, full text] - https://www.nami.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/BOD_OPs_Business_Support_Relationships.pdf
- 8.6.2.1: sponsorship = "unrestricted educational grants of financial or in-kind support for a specific NAMI activity or program ... Typically, a sponsorship provides for recognition of the entity providing the support."
- 8.6.2.3 cause marketing: sponsor "may gain public recognition for such public support of NAMI, but may not tie this support to any commercial return or other financial benefit to the sponsor."
- 8.6.3.1 Endorsement (excluded): "NAMI will not endorse, license, certify, or in any way imply support for any sponsor's product, service or program, including recognizing or certifying the quality or standards of any particular product, service, or program."
- 8.6.3.2 Product association: "NAMI's name and logo may not be associated with specific branded products."
- 8.6.4 #3: funds for communication projects accepted "only when it has final editorial approval over content and distribution." #4: "NAMI will neither endorse nor accept conditions that allow the perception that NAMI endorses any products, devices, treatments or services NAMI may require a disclaimer explicitly stating this policy be included in any publication." #9: use of NAMI's name and logo not produced by NAMI "is not permitted without specific, prior written permission."
- Public statement: "As a matter of policy, NAMI does not endorse any specific treatment or service." and "NAMI state organizations and affiliates are separate entities and where appropriate are established independently as non-profit 501c3 organizations." [READ via browser-UA fetch] - https://www.nami.org/who-we-are/our-finances/major-foundation-corporate-sponsorships/
- Website terms: "not necessarily those of NAMI or endorsed by NAMI"; outbound links "are not intended as an endorsement by NAMI of the organization or individual operating the website" [READ] - https://www.nami.org/Terms-of-Use/Disclaimer
- NAMI HelpLine resource directory disclaimer reported as "not endorsed by NAMI, and NAMI is not responsible for the content of or service provided by any of these resources" [SNIPPET, not re-confirmed in page text] - https://nami.org/helpline-resource-directory
- Affiliate-level examples found: NAMI Multnomah resource guide: "Their inclusion in this resource guide is for information purposes only and is not intended to represent an endorsement by NAMI Multnomah." [READ] (URL in section 2). NAMI Central Oregon's own pages carry only a non-verification note ("Some community offers ... have not been independently verified") - https://namicentraloregon.org/community-resources-and-support-after-the-silver-rail-elementary-tragedy/ ; no "does not endorse" wording found on its sponsorship/resources pages - NOT FOUND.
- Not found on any benchmark sponsor page I read (Yolo, Davidson, NAMI Oregon, SW Washington): a sponsor-specific non-endorsement line. [INFER] The 8.6.4 #4 clause ("may require a disclaimer ... in any publication") is the nearest published template.

### 4.1 Pattern by price point [INFER - my tally across A-G]
- **~$1,000 (tiers $500-$1,500):** Appears in 5 of 7 programmes (A, B, C, F, G). Website name/logo in 4 of 5; social/newsletter mention in 4 of 5; event-day signage/programme name in 3 of 5; exhibit table or tickets in 3 of 5. Print-style extras (programme ad, T-shirt name) only where the org has such a product. Nothing is personalised.
- **~$2,500 (tiers $2,000-$2,500):** In 5 of 7 (A, B, C, E, F). All give event/signage recognition; website logo in 4 of 5; social/newsletter or thank-you in 4 of 5; and **some form of access** in 5 of 5 (exhibit table, 10 tickets, a hosted "playdate", or a private tour). Logo on poster/press only at the higher end.
- **~$5,000 (tiers $5,000):** In 6 of 7 (A, B, C, D, E, G). Event-level recognition in all six; website listing/logo in all six; exhibit table, tickets (2-10) or a hosted experience in 4 of 6 (A, B, C, E); press-release mention in 2 of 6 (A name only; C dedicated); the $5k level is the **top tier** at the two smallest orgs (C, G), i.e. "Presenting" status.
- **$10,000+ (tiers $10k-$20k):** In 4 of 7 (A, B, D, E). Distinguishing items: "Presented by"/top billing or Gold-at-both-events (A, B, D); a featured newsletter/e-news spotlight (D, E); tickets (B 20, E 4-8); a one-off dedicated press release, speaking slot or co-branded webinar (A only, Presenting/Premier). (A website testimonial quote appears already at F's $2k tier.) Caps ("limit two") are common on the top tier (D, E).
- **Annual vs per-event:** programmes D and E (annual) bundle recognition at two signature events and add a year-round web/newsletter placement; per-event programmes (A, B, C, G) price by event. [INFER] A one-person team is more likely to sustain the annual model if each tier = a fixed set of placements (web listing, newsletter line, social thank-you, event-day sign/programme line) reused across events, as D and F do.
