# Sponsorship staff playbook

The Regional Director’s first job is to bring in the first group of businesses. Use the microsite to share relevant offers and review new inquiries and signed commitments. Keep follow-up dates in Monday.com if that is already your routine, and continue using Little Green Light and the organization’s accounting workflow for gifts, payments and receipts as appropriate. Keep agreements, sponsor assets and fulfillment evidence in approved storage.

## Invite the first businesses

1. Start with 8 to 12 businesses you know or contacts who have agreed to a warm introduction. Choose one reason the offer fits and one suggested level for each.
2. Add the business to the staff prospect view and copy its personal proposal link. Check that it opens the expected selection. Email the link and the current sponsor PDF at `/api/guide.pdf` from the organization’s account. Let the contact choose a different cause, request a conversation, or sign a standard commitment.
3. Follow up once after seven days in the same thread. Stop the sequence when someone declines or asks to stop. Use the [Resource Guide launch plan](resource-guide-launch.md) to pace outreach around the available placements.

## From conversation to renewal

| When | In the staff view | Director’s action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inquiry received | Open the existing inquiry and review the business interest and notes. | Reply with the best-fit option or agree on a short conversation. Confirm capacity before promising recognition. |
| Signed commitment | Open the submitted record and invoice. Check the sponsor’s selections, terms and recognition permission. | In a live pilot, confirm capacity and send the approved invoice through the accounting workflow. Record the invoice reference and send date. Payment is due in 30 days. |
| Invoice overdue | Review the overdue queue against the accounting ledger. | Send one reminder after checking for incoming payments or an approved extension. Add any agreed follow-up date to the record. |
| Payment verified | Record payment and review the welcome draft and benefit schedule. | Match funds in the ledger before recording payment. Send the welcome with the next recognition dates. Confirm name spelling, logo quality, link and permission before publishing. Mark active after onboarding. |
| Benefit delivered | Tick the benefit and log its URL, issue date, print proof or event reference in the note. | Combine recognition in the next available channel or print batch. If a stronger placement covers an inherited benefit in the same channel, document that fulfillment once for both items. Honor private recognition preferences. |
| About a month before annual renewal | Review the renewal queue and the original choices. | Offer the same level at the current published price. Reuse the contact and recognition details after confirming changes, then get a fresh agreement and invoice. Close the expiring record when the next term starts or the sponsor lapses. |

The microsite provides an invoice after a standard commitment and prepares email drafts. Send email from the organization’s account and arrange payment privately through the approved accounting workflow. Check the ledger before recording payment and issue receipts through the accounting system.

## Batch the work

- **Weekly · 15 minutes** — Review inquiries, signed commitments and due follow-ups. Open `/admin/conversions` to check visits, builder starts, submitted inquiries and signed commitments over the last 30 days. Check payments and print deadlines separately, and release any reservation you have confirmed will lapse.
- **Monthly · 45 to 75 minutes** — Publish one website update and batch newsletter, social and approved project credit. Review the next month’s benefit queue and schedule any employee presentations in existing presentation slots. Confirm the batch evidence before marking work complete.
- **Quarterly · 30 minutes** — Review prospects, cash received, benefits due and upcoming renewals. Confirm availability before making new offers. Count an employee presentation as a separate scheduled block of about 90 minutes in addition to routine administration.

Use the acquisition counts to adjust outreach. For a sponsor update, select **Quarterly partner update** in the email templates. The draft includes recognition recorded in the past 90 days and the organization’s published reach figures. Check the figures, add a relevant newsletter link if available, and send the reviewed message from your usual mailbox.

## Recognition calendar

| Window | Typical work |
| --- | --- |
| After payment | Welcome, partner badge and announcement kit where included. Confirm sponsor assets and permissions. |
| Within two weeks where promised | Publish eligible website listings or logo placements. |
| At scheduled production dates | Check sponsor credits in guide print and digital proofs, directory updates, episodes, classes and events. Use the agreed deadline or issue date when the production schedule differs from the generic queue. |
| At the next scheduled newsletter, social or impact report batch | Use the tiers and fulfillment queue to confirm the format and how many appearances remain. For multi-issue benefits, list the issue dates in the sponsor record and mark complete after the last issue. |
| Ahead of an annual term end | Send the renewal invitation and schedule removal or refresh of dated recognition. Confirm the applicable season or edition for project and NAMIWalks repeat sponsorships. |

## Email templates

Open the built-in draft for outreach, invoice, overdue, welcome or renewal. Replace all bracketed fields in the examples below. Review prices, dates, sponsor preferences and recipient before sending. Invoice links belong in private sponsor correspondence.

**Invite · Help a Central Oregon family find local support**

> Hi [name],  
> [Personal connection or reason this fits the business.] We’re preparing a printed and digital guide for Central Oregon families looking for mental health care. Would [business] consider helping get it into their hands? [Level] is [amount] and includes [credit]. I’ve opened that option for you at [personal proposal link]. The [sponsor PDF] is easy to share with your team. You can choose another option or reply if you’d like to talk.
>
> [Director name], NAMI Central Oregon

**Invoice · Sponsorship invoice [invoice number]**

> Hi [name],  
> Thank you for choosing [selections]. The total is [amount], due [date]. Here’s your invoice with payment instructions: [approved invoice link]. Please reply if your accounts payable team needs a different billing contact.  
> [Director name]

**Payment follow-up · Checking on invoice [invoice number]**

> Hi [name],  
> I’m checking on the [amount] sponsorship invoice due [date]. Could you let me know when we should expect payment? If it has cleared, please share the date and reference so I can match it.  
> [Director name]

**Welcome · Thank you for backing [project or annual partnership]**

> Hi [name],  
> We received your [amount] payment. Thank you for supporting [selections]. We’ve recorded your recognition preference as [preference]. Your next steps from us are [benefits and dates]. If public recognition is selected, please confirm that [display name, link and logo] are correct by [proof deadline].  
> [Director name]

**Renewal · Continue your Central Oregon partnership**

> Hi [name],  
> Thank you for helping make [program or benefit] possible this year. Your annual term ends [date]. Would [business] like to continue at [level] for [current price]? Reply with any changes to your contact or recognition details and I’ll send the next agreement.  
> [Director name]
